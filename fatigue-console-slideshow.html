The Hard Question
If we intervene by moving a critical task to another astronaut — are we reducing risk, or simply moving it somewhere else?
Why This Matters
Identifying fatigue is only half the problem. Interventions can displace risk across the crew and across time — creating new hazards downstream. That is the problem Fatigue Console is built to explore.
